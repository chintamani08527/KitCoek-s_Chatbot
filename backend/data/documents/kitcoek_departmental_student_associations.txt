OVERVIEW
At KIT's College of Engineering (Autonomous), Kolhapur (KITCoEK), every engineering department maintains an active, student-driven departmental association. These associations bridge academic curricula with real-world engineering practices, hardware prototyping, software design, and industrial competitions.

During PIONEER 2025 (KITCoEK's premier National Level Technical Symposium), each departmental association organizes signature flagship competitions, drawing participants from engineering institutions across Maharashtra and India.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MECHANICAL ENGINEERING — MESA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Association Name: Mechanical Engineering Students' Association (MESA)
- Department: Mechanical Engineering
- Category: Departmental Association
- Tagline: Pioneering Mechanical Innovation, CAD Design & Engineering Excellence
- Verified PIONEER 2025 Flagship Event: "Battle of Wits" (Departmental Technical Competition)
- About & Purpose:
  MESA encourages students to explore mechanical engineering through practical activities, technical competitions, workshops, and project exhibitions. MESA bridges classroom theoretical mechanics with real-world manufacturing, automotive design, robotics, and fluid mechanics.
- Core Activities:
  * Battle of Wits — Flagship departmental technical event listed under MESA at PIONEER 2025
  * Computer-Aided Design (CAD) and 3D modeling competitions using SolidWorks, CATIA, and AutoCAD
  * Mechanical model-making, robotics exhibitions, and technical project displays
  * Hands-on workshops on modern manufacturing tools, CNC machining, thermodynamics, and industrial visits
- Official Links:
  * Official Instagram: Search Instagram for MESA KITCoEK (https://www.instagram.com/)
  * Official Page: https://www.kitcoek.in/studentClubs
- Student Benefits:
  * Develop practical engineering and mechanical design skills.
  * Learn about machines, manufacturing technologies, and advanced CAD/CAM software.
  * Improve teamwork, technical presentation, and leadership qualities.
  * Build tangible prototypes to strengthen technical portfolios for core mechanical placements.
