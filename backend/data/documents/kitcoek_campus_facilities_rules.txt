CAMPUS FACILITIES & TIMINGS:
1. Central Library:
   - Working Hours: Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
   - Reading Hall: Extended till 11:00 PM during mid-term and end-term examinations.
   - Facilities: E-Library with IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect access; Book Bank facility for SC/ST and economically backward students.
2. CBCR (Central Book & Computer Resource / Career Building Cell):
   - Located at Administrative Block Ground Floor (Room ADM-05).
   - Handles printing of study materials, bonafide certificates, ID card re-issuance, and course packets.
3. Central Canteen & Cafeteria:
   - Operating Hours: 7:30 AM to 7:00 PM daily.
   - Separate dining seating for faculty and 400+ students. Hygienic RO water dispensers installed.
4. Health Center / Medical Clinic:
   - Resident medical doctor available from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the Gymkhana building. Free first aid and emergency ambulance service available 24x7.
5. Gymkhana & Sports Complex:
   - Features standard football/cricket ground, 400m running track, badminton courts, basketball court, and indoor gym with modern fitness equipment.

RULES & REGULATIONS:
1. Campus Dress Code & ID Cards:
   - Students must wear college uniform on designated days (Monday and Thursday).
   - Valid Identity Card must be displayed visibly around the neck at all times inside campus.
2. Anti-Ragging Policy:
   - Zero tolerance to ragging under Maharashtra Prohibition of Ragging Act 1999. Strict disciplinary action including rustication and FIR.
3. Vehicle Parking:
   - Two-wheeler parking at Gate No. 2; Four-wheelers prohibited for students without special administrative parking pass.
4. Scholarship & Financial Aid Deadlines:
   - MahaDBT government scholarship applications submission deadline: October 30 / November 15 each academic cycle.
   - Required documents: Caste Certificate, Caste Validity, Income Certificate (from Tehsildar), Domicile Certificate, and Bank Passbook linked with Aadhaar.
