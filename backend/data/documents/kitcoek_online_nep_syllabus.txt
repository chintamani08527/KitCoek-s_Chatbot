DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND BUSINESS SYSTEMS (CSBS) — NEP-2020 SYLLABUS DIRECTORY:
Source: Official College Online Syllabus Portal (https://www.kitcoek.in/online-syllabus)
Curriculum Schemes: 2023 [NEP-2020] Scheme & 2021 Cycle-2 Autonomy Scheme

Overview:
Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) is a cutting-edge 4-year undergraduate B.Tech program crafted in alignment with National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and industry requirements (Business 4.0). It blends rigorous core computer science with enterprise management, data science, financial analytics, and emerging technologies.

1. CSBS All-Years Complete Curriculum Structure (NEP-2020):
   - Official Structure PDF: https://kitcoek.s3.amazonaws.com/syllabus/CSBS_ALL_Year%20Structure.pdf
   - Breakdown: Full 8-semester credit system, course codes, teaching and evaluation scheme (ISE, MSE, ESE), program outcomes (POs), and multidisciplinary minors.

2. First Year (F.Y. B.Tech CSBS) — 2023 [NEP-2020]:
