ABOUT MERAKI:
Meraki is KITCOEK's premier flagship annual cultural festival, student gathering, and campus carnival.
It is the pinnacle of student life at Kolhapur Institute of Technology, bringing together thousands of students,
faculty, alumni, and guest performers for multiple days of electrifying cultural competitions, concerts, and celebrations.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS OF MERAKI:
• Dance & Choreography Competitions (Solo, Duet, and Mega Group Dance)
• Musical Concerts, Band Wars, and Open-Mic Nights
• Dramatic Arts, Theatrical Performances & Street Plays (Nukkad Natak)
• Fashion Show / Traditional & Western Ramp Walk
• Celebrity Guest Shows & DJ Night
• Traditional Days, Theme Days, and Cultural Exhibitions
• Annual Awards Ceremony honoring academic, technical, sports, and cultural achievers

HOW TO PARTICIPATE OR JOIN MERAKI TEAMS:
Students interested in performing, anchoring, organizing, backstage management, stage lighting, sound, or media coverage
can join through the Cultural Club (Cultural Committee) during the odd semester and participate in auditions announced by the committee.
